    Your daily training call

    Each morning you log sleep, soreness and energy. Today's Call tells you whether to rest, go hypertrophy, or go heavy, builds the workout with suggested weights, tracks your lifts, and times your meals around training.

    Before you start

    Today's Call gives general fitness guidance. It isn't medical advice and doesn't know your health history. Talk to a doctor before starting a new program, and stop any exercise that causes pain.

    Switching units converts the numbers you see; saved lifts are converted too.
    Renaming a day starts that day's exercise history fresh. Your logged lifts are kept.

    Your data lives only on this phone. Save a backup now and then, and use it to move to a new phone.

    About

    Today's Call gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Readiness calls and weight suggestions are rules of thumb; listen to your body. Protein guidance follows the International Society of Sports Nutrition position stands.
